Privacy Policy

Retro Toolbox · Effective October 29, 2026

The short version. Retro Toolbox has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no trackers. It does not collect personal data and sends nothing about you to the developer. Your files stay on your device. The app only connects to the internet for the specific features described below, and only when you use them.

Who we are

Retro Toolbox is an open-source app published by Gabriel Moterani. The source code is available at github.com/hiitsgabe/retro_toolbox. Questions about this policy: hi@gabeismy.name.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your files. The app reads and writes the files and folders you open in it (for example to extract an archive, convert a disc image or patch a game file you own). They are processed locally and never uploaded.
  • Settings. Preferences such as your library folders, theme, the content sources you add and your server settings are stored in the app's private storage on the device.
  • Caches. Downloaded artwork and sports data are cached locally so they are not fetched again. Clearing the app's storage removes them.

When the app connects to the internet

Each connection below happens only when you use that feature. Like any internet request, the server involved sees your IP address; none of them receives your name, an account or an identifier for you.

  • Update checks. The app asks the project's GitHub release page for the latest version. The Google Play version updates through Google Play instead.
  • Artwork. Box art and console logos are fetched from public artwork servers when a view needs them.
  • Sports rosters. The roster patcher downloads current teams and players from public sports data services (such as ESPN) for the season and league you choose.
  • Content sources you add. If you add your own catalog or source URLs in the app, the app contacts those servers on your behalf. Their privacy practices are their own.
  • Sharing by link. When you explicitly choose to share something as a link, that item is uploaded to an ephemeral file host (0x0.st) and the link is given to you. Nothing is uploaded unless you tap share.

Local network servers

The app can run SMB, FTP and WebDAV servers to share folders you pick with other devices on your local network. The servers run only while you keep them on, expose only the folders you selected, and are not reachable from the internet unless you configure your own network that way.

Permissions on Android

  • All files access. Retro Toolbox is a file-management tool: it needs real file paths to browse, move, extract, convert and patch files in the folders you choose, including SD cards and USB drives. It only touches folders you open in the app.
  • Notifications. Long tasks (downloads, extractions, conversions, running servers) show their progress in a notification while the app is in the background.
  • Internet. For the features listed above.

Children

Retro Toolbox is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. The history is visible in the project's Git repository.
