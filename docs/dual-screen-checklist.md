# Dual screen on the AYN Thor

Run this on the device after installing a build. Nothing here can be checked
on an emulator or a single-screen phone: the companion needs a second display
that is on.

Go through the items in order. Each says what to do and what should happen.

1. **The card.** Open Settings. A *Dual screen* card is there, with *Off*,
   *Main on top* and *Main on bottom*. On a single-screen device, or on the
   Thor with the bottom screen off, the card is not there.
2. **Main on top.** Choose *Main on top*. The bottom screen shows the Task
   Manager. The controller still moves focus on the top screen, and nothing
   on the bottom screen takes it. If the controller stops moving focus on the
   top screen right after the bottom one opens, even for a moment, note it.
   Then touch the bottom screen and press the d-pad: focus still moves on the
   top screen at once, and no "isn't responding" dialog shows within 10 s.
3. **Details follow the cursor.** Open a console's game list and move through
   the games with the d-pad. The bottom screen shows the details of the game
   under the cursor within a moment. Touch scrolls the details.
4. **Tap a game.** On the game list, tap a game that is not the one on the
   bottom screen. Its page does not open: the bottom screen shows it, and its
   card on the top screen gets an outline. Tap the same game again: its page
   opens. Do the same in the grid view and in cover flow, where swiping to
   another game also changes the bottom screen.
5. **Buttons on the bottom screen.** With a game shown there, tap *Download*
   on the bottom screen. The download starts as if pressed on the top screen
   (the same confirmation, if one shows, appears on the top screen), and the
   bottom screen's status and buttons follow it: *Pause*, *Cancel*.
6. **Tasks follow you out.** Leave the game list. The bottom screen shows the
   same Task Manager as the one Select opens, with its five tabs and counts.
   Start a download and watch its progress there. Tap its pause, cancel and
   favourite buttons on the bottom screen: each works as on the top screen.
7. **Main on bottom.** Choose *Main on bottom*. The app restarts on the
   bottom screen and the companion opens on the top one.
8. **Home and back.** Press Home, then open the app again. The companion
   closes while the app is away and comes back with it.
9. **Screen off and on.** Long-press the AYN button to turn the bottom screen
   off, then on again. The app does not crash, and the companion comes back
   once the screen is on.
10. **When something fails.** Settings shows "Second screen unavailable". Note
    which step it was. If *Main on bottom* fails once, choose the mode again:
    the app only refuses to move to the same screen twice in a row on its
    own, and a choice made by hand always tries again.
